BM Academy — Free Lessons

Learn the craft — or hire me to do it.

1 · The 3-Second Hook

Viewers decide in the first 3 seconds. Start with your most exciting frame — the result, the shock, the question. Put the payoff first, then explain, like movie trailers do.

2 · Cut on the Beat

Random cuts feel amateur. Place cuts on beat drops — in CapCut, the audio waveform spikes are your cut points. Cut on the big beats, not every beat.

3 · Captions for the Silent Majority

Most people watch with sound off. Bold, word-by-word kinetic captions in the centre of the frame — 3–5 words per screen, key words highlighted.

4 · One Clear Button (Websites)

Every page needs ONE obvious action: Call Now, WhatsApp Us, Book a Table. Make it big, repeat it after every section — and add a sticky WhatsApp button on mobile.

5 · Demand First, Product Second

Check search demand before anything else. Boring products with steady demand beat exciting products with no demand, every time.

6 · Consistency Beats Perfection

3–5 solid posts a week beats one masterpiece a month. "Done and posted" always beats "perfect and in drafts."

My Story

From curiosity to a one-person creative studio.

How it started

Like most great stories, mine started with curiosity. I picked up video editing because I was fascinated by how a few cuts, some music, and the right pacing could turn ordinary footage into something people couldn't stop watching. I spent countless nights learning — transitions, color grading, sound design, storytelling — one skill at a time.

Then came the Reels era. Short-form video exploded, and I was ready. I edited Reels, Shorts, and TikToks, learning what makes people stop scrolling: the hook in the first 3 seconds, cuts on the beat, captions that pop.

Where it led

I noticed small businesses around me struggling with the same problem: they needed an online presence but couldn't afford agencies. So I learned web development — clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — and started building modern, animated, mobile-friendly websites that look like they cost ten times more.

Then came AI. I built chatbots that answer customers around the clock — on WhatsApp and Instagram — and added AI chatbots and QR digital menus to my services. Today, BM Digital covers what every modern business needs: a website that impresses, videos that attract, and bots that never sleep. I'm still learning every single day — and the journey is just getting started.
